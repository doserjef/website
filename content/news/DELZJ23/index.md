Check out our new paper showcasing a new method for estimating spatially varying occupancy trends within `spOccupancy`. We apply the framework to inform monitoring and conservation assessments of 11 anuran species in Minnesota, USA. See the [paper here](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1365-2664.70342) for more details.
