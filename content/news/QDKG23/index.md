A fun new paper led by Gabriela Quinlan at Penn State University was just published in Science of the Total Environment. Using a dataset from Maryland and joint species distribution models implemented in spAbundance, we provide the largest scale, most phylogenetically resolved assessment of non-native honey bee density effects on wild bee abundance to date. See the [paper here](https://jeffdoser.com/files/pubs/quinlan2024STOTEN.pdf) for more details.
