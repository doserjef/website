A new paper led by Ben Goldstein provides guidance to practitioners on when to use data integration for a species distribution modeling application. We provide a variety of practical considerations for researchers, students, and practitioners to consider the pros and cons of data integration. See the [paper here](https://doserlab.com/publication/gdpp24/) for more details.
