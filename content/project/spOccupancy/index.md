I am the lead author and maintainer of `spOccupancy`, an R package designed to fit spatially-explicit single-species, multi-species, and integrated occupancy models. See [the package website](https://www.jeffdoser.com/files/spoccupancy-web/) for information on package functionality, detailed vignettes, and complete documentation. I am currently working on a series of extensions to the package's functionality including: 

1. Random intercepts in single-species integrated occupancy models
2. Multi-species integrated occupancy models (i.e., integrated community occupancy models)
3. Spatially-varying coefficient models
4. Dynamic models
